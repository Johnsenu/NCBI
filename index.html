About NCBI

New Creature Bible Institute (NCBI) is the training arm of New Creature Evangelical Church, founded over 30 years ago under the leadership of the late Dr. E. J. Oludayitan, alongside a few other committed men and women of God. The Institute's pioneer registrar was Dr. Julius Asorose, who now serves as Chairman of the Board of Directors. Since its founding, NCBI has grown to offer structured theological training — from Diploma to Doctorate level — raising ministers and kingdom workers under its enduring motto.

"The Whole Word to the Whole World"

Frequently Asked Questions

What programs does NCBI offer?

NCBI offers four levels of theological training: Diploma in Theology, Bachelor's Degree in Theology, Master's Degree in Theology, and Doctorate in Theology (coming soon).

How do I register for a program?

Click "Register Now" or "Get Started" on this page to begin your registration for any currently open program.

Is there an entry requirement to enroll?

Entry requirements vary by program level. Contact the registrar's office for specific requirements for your program of interest.

Are classes held online or in person?

NCBI offers three flexible class formats to suit every student:

  • In-Person Days: Attend a physical classroom for hands-on sessions, group work, and community events.
  • Online Days: Study independently from home through recorded lectures and reading assignments.
  • Hybrid Days: Join a live class where some students are physically present while others attend via video call.

How long does each program take to complete?

  • Diploma in Theology: 2 years
  • Bachelor's Degree in Theology: 2 years
  • Master's Degree in Theology: 1 year
  • Doctorate in Theology: 3–4 years (program details to be announced)

Will I receive a certificate upon completion?

Yes, graduates receive an official certificate/degree upon successful completion of their program.

Who do I contact for more information?

You can reach the NCBI registrar's office through the contact details in the footer of this page.
